Community News · 01

Shop Owners Raise Alarm Over Rising Burglaries

Business owners around Penoks Bustop Plaza, Herbatex, and Almost Inland, particularly in the vicinity of Ada Negbu Azu Shop, are expressing growing concern over a series of burglaries affecting shops in the area.

The repeated incidents have heightened fears among traders, many of whom depend on their businesses for their daily livelihood. The situation has raised questions about the effectiveness of existing security arrangements and the need for more practical measures to protect businesses.

Shop owners are urging community stakeholders to treat the situation with urgency and work towards ensuring a safer environment for businesses to operate.

Community News · 02

Landlords Meet Over Security Concerns as Burglaries Persist

Following repeated reports of shop burglaries around Penoks Bustop Plaza, Herbatex, and Almost Inland, landlords in the affected areas have held meetings to discuss ways to address the growing security concerns.

Despite these discussions, residents and business owners remain concerned that little progress has been made towards implementing effective solutions. Reports of further incidents have continued to fuel frustration among those affected.

There are growing calls for the meetings to produce concrete action, with suggestions for improved security coordination, better monitoring of vulnerable areas, and closer collaboration with security agencies.

Community News · 03

Community Seeks Urgent Action to Curb Shop Burglaries

The continued reports of burglaries targeting shops around Penoks Bustop Plaza, Herbatex, and Almost Inland have prompted renewed calls for decisive action to address insecurity in the community.

While landlords have reportedly met to consider possible solutions, the persistence of the incidents has left many business owners questioning when tangible steps will be taken to safeguard their properties.

Community members are calling on landlords, traders, residents, and relevant security authorities to work together on a coordinated response. Proposed measures include improved night-time surveillance, better lighting in vulnerable locations, and prompt reporting of suspicious activities.

A united and sustained effort will be essential to strengthening local security and restoring confidence among business owners and residents.
